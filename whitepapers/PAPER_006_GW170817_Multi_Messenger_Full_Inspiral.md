# Paper #6: Multi-Messenger GW170817 Full Inspiral

## Abstract
This whitepaper presents the results of a full inspiral simulation for the multi-messenger event GW170817, highlighting key aspects of gravitational waves and electromagnetic signals detected from the event.

## Introduction
The full inspiral simulation spans 100 seconds, with a frequency range from 23 Hz to 300 Hz. The chirp time for the gravitational waves is approximately 113 seconds, which allows for a detailed analysis of the inspiral phase leading up to the merger.

## UQFF Damping Factors
The damping factors used in the simulation include:
- Aether: 1.0
- SCm: 1.0
- TRZ: 0.9

- Combined: 0.333

## Waveform Statistics
The waveform statistics show:
- Peak GR: 5.8791 × 10⁻¹⁷
- Peak UQFF: 1.9596 × 10⁻¹⁷
- Reduction: 66.7%

## SNR Analysis
Comparing the signal-to-noise ratios (SNR):
- GR SNR: 32.4
- UQFF SNR: 10.8
- Reduction: 66.7%
Both signals are detectable, indicating their significance in multi-messenger astronomy.
