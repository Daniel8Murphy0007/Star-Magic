# Paper #10: Post-Merger Oscillations and Remnant Mass in UQFF

## Abstract

Binary mergers produce remnants that oscillate before settling into stable configurations. We analyze post-merger dynamics in the Unified Quantum Field Framework (UQFF), focusing on neutron star remnant oscillations and black hole ringdown. For GW170817, UQFF predicts extended hypermassive neutron star (HMNS) lifetimes due to reduced energy loss (τ_HMNS = 9× τ_GR), enabling detection of f-mode oscillations at 2-3 kHz. For black hole mergers, UQFF increases final remnant mass by 0.15 M☉ due to suppressed gravitational wave emission, shifting ringdown frequency by Δf/f ~ -3.7%. We derive quasi-normal mode (QNM) frequency corrections and remnant mass formulas, providing testable predictions for third-generation detectors.

## 1. Introduction

Binary mergers produce remnants exhibiting characteristic oscillations:

**BNS Mergers:**
- Hypermassive NS (HMNS) with f-mode oscillations at 2-3 kHz
- Lifetime τ ~ 10-100 ms before collapse to BH

**BBH Mergers:**
- Black hole ringdown via quasi-normal modes (QNMs)

UQFF damping (D_total < 1) extends remnant lifetimes and modifies oscillation frequencies through energy retention.

## 2. HMNS Lifetime Extension

### 2.1 Energy Loss Rate

UQFF reduces radiated power:
**P_UQFF = D²_total × P_GR**

For GW170817 (D_total = 0.333):
**P_UQFF = 0.111 × P_GR** (89% reduction)

### 2.2 Extended Lifetime

**τ_HMNS,UQFF = τ_GR / D²_total = 9.0 × τ_GR**

For typical τ_GR ~ 50 ms:
**τ_UQFF ~ 450 ms** (9× longer)

**Observable:** Extended 2-3 kHz emission lasting ~0.5 seconds

## 3. Black Hole Ringdown

### 3.1 Remnant Mass Excess

From Paper #5 validation:
- M_tot = 65 M☉ (GW150914-like)
- E_radiated,GR = 3.0 M☉
- E_radiated,UQFF = 0.65 M☉ (D² = 0.66)
- **ΔM_retained = 2.35 M☉**

**M_f,UQFF = 64.35 M☉ vs M_f,GR = 62.0 M☉**

### 3.2 QNM Frequency Shift

Frequency scales as:
**f_QNM ∝ 1 / M_f**

**f_UQFF / f_GR = M_GR / M_UQFF = 62.0 / 64.35 = 0.963**

For GW150914 (f_obs = 251 Hz):
**f_UQFF = 242 Hz** (Δf = -9 Hz, -3.7% shift)

**Within current measurement uncertainty (±8 Hz)**
