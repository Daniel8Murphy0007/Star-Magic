**Here is as comprehensive a list of scientific constants as possible, drawn from authoritative sources like the 2022 CODATA/NIST recommended values (the international standard for physics/chemistry) and Wikipedia's curated lists.** There is no single exhaustive catalog "across all science"—physical constants number in the low hundreds when including all derived, particle-specific, and physico-chemical values; mathematical constants have dozens of named ones (with hundreds more specialized/obscure); and other fields (chemistry, biology, astronomy) mostly use derived or empirical constants rather than new universal ones. Many physics constants became exact in the 2019 SI redefinition.

I've organized them into categories for readability, focusing on the most recognized and fundamental ones with symbols, approximate/precise values (using 2022 CODATA where available), units, and notes. Values use scientific notation where helpful; exact ones (post-2019) have zero uncertainty.

### 1. Universal Physical Constants (CODATA 2022)
These are the bedrock constants.

- **Speed of light in vacuum** \( c \): exactly \( 299\,792\,458 \) m/s
- **Planck constant** \( h \): exactly \( 6.626\,070\,15 \times 10^{-34} \) J⋅Hz⁻¹ (or J⋅s)
- **Reduced Planck constant** \( \hbar = h / 2\pi \): exactly \( 1.054\,571\,817 \times 10^{-34} \) J⋅s
- **Boltzmann constant** \( k \) or \( k_B \): exactly \( 1.380\,649 \times 10^{-23} \) J⋅K⁻¹
- **Newtonian constant of gravitation** \( G \): \( 6.674\,30(15) \times 10^{-11} \) m³⋅kg⁻¹⋅s⁻² (relative uncertainty ~2.2 × 10⁻⁵)
- **Cosmological constant** \( \Lambda \): ~\( 1.089(29) \times 10^{-52} \) m⁻² (or slight variant ~1.088(30) × 10⁻⁵² m⁻²)

### 2. Electromagnetic and Quantum Constants
- **Elementary charge** \( e \): exactly \( 1.602\,176\,634 \times 10^{-19} \) C
- **Fine-structure constant** \( \alpha \): \( 0.007\,297\,352\,5643(11) \) (≈ 1/137.035999177)
- **Inverse fine-structure constant** \( \alpha^{-1} \): \( 137.035\,999\,177(21) \)
- **Vacuum electric permittivity** \( \varepsilon_0 \): \( 8.854\,187\,8188(14) \times 10^{-12} \) F⋅m⁻¹
- **Vacuum magnetic permeability** \( \mu_0 \): \( 1.256\,637\,061\,27(20) \times 10^{-6} \) N⋅A⁻²
- **Characteristic impedance of vacuum** \( Z_0 \): \( 376.730\,313\,412(59) \) Ω
- **Conductance quantum** \( G_0 = 2e^2/h \): exactly \( 7.748\,091\,729 \times 10^{-5} \) S
- **Inverse conductance quantum**: exactly \( 12\,906.403\,72\ldots \) Ω
- **von Klitzing constant** \( R_K = h/e^2 \): exactly \( 25\,812.807\,45\ldots \) Ω
- **Josephson constant** \( K_J = 2e/h \): exactly \( 483\,597.8484\ldots \times 10^9 \) Hz⋅V⁻¹
- **Magnetic flux quantum** \( \Phi_0 = h/2e \): exactly \( 2.067\,833\,848 \times 10^{-15} \) Wb

### 3. Radiation and Thermodynamic Constants
- **Stefan–Boltzmann constant** \( \sigma \): exactly \( 5.670\,374\,419 \times 10^{-8} \) W⋅m⁻²⋅K⁻⁴
- **First radiation constant** \( c_1 \): exactly \( 3.741\,771\,852 \times 10^{-16} \) W⋅m²
- **First radiation constant for spectral radiance** \( c_{1L} \): exactly \( 1.191\,042\,972 \times 10^{-16} \) W⋅m²⋅sr⁻¹
- **Second radiation constant** \( c_2 \): exactly \( 1.438\,776\,877 \times 10^{-2} \) m⋅K
- **Wien wavelength displacement law constant** \( b \): exactly \( 2.897\,771\,955 \times 10^{-3} \) m⋅K
- **Wien frequency displacement law constant** \( b' \): exactly \( 5.878\,925\,757 \times 10^{10} \) Hz⋅K⁻¹
- **Wien entropy displacement law constant** \( b_{\text{entropy}} \): exactly \( 3.002\,916\,077 \times 10^{-3} \) m⋅K

### 4. Particle Masses and Ratios (Selected from CODATA)
- **Electron mass** \( m_e \): \( 9.109\,383\,7139(28) \times 10^{-31} \) kg
- **Muon mass** \( m_\mu \): \( 1.883\,531\,627(42) \times 10^{-28} \) kg
- **Tau mass** \( m_\tau \): \( 3.167\,54(21) \times 10^{-27} \) kg
- **Proton mass** \( m_p \): \( 1.672\,621\,925\,95(52) \times 10^{-27} \) kg
- **Neutron mass** \( m_n \): \( 1.674\,927\,500\,56(85) \times 10^{-27} \) kg
- **Alpha particle mass**: \( 6.644\,657\,3450 \times 10^{-27} \) kg (with full set of ratios like alpha-electron mass ratio ≈ 7294.299 541 71)
- Many more ratios (e.g., proton-to-electron \( m_p/m_e \approx 1836.15267343 \), electron-muon mass ratio, etc.) and derived masses/energies in MeV/u.

### 5. Physico-Chemical and Atomic Constants (Selected)
These include (exact where redefined):
- **Avogadro constant** \( N_A \): exactly \( 6.022\,140\,76 \times 10^{23} \) mol⁻¹
- **Molar gas constant** \( R \): exactly \( 8.314\,462\,618 \) J⋅mol⁻¹⋅K⁻¹
- **Faraday constant** \( F = N_A e \): exactly \( 96\,485.3321 \) C⋅mol⁻¹
- **Molar volume of ideal gas** (at STP, various conditions)
- Atomic units (e.g., Bohr radius \( a_0 \), Hartree energy, etc.)
- Rydberg constant, Bohr magneton, nuclear magneton, Compton wavelengths, Thomson cross section, and dozens of particle-specific (electron g-factor, muon anomaly, etc.)

**Full CODATA 2022 list**: The NIST complete ASCII table has **over 100–150 entries** (universal, electromagnetic, atomic/nuclear, physico-chemical, energy equivalents, conventional values, etc.), including every ratio, conversion, and derived quantity. See the official source for the exhaustive version: physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/.

### 6. Mathematical Constants (from Wikipedia's List)
These are exact by mathematical definition (not measured). Wikipedia's main list includes:

- **Zero**: 0 (additive identity)
- **One**: 1 (multiplicative identity)
- **Two**: 2
- **One half**: 1/2
- **Pi** \( \pi \): ≈ 3.1415926535... (circle circumference/diameter)
- **Tau** \( \tau = 2\pi \): ≈ 6.283185307... (circle circumference/radius)
- **Square root of 2** \( \sqrt{2} \) (Pythagoras' constant): ≈ 1.414213562...
- **Square root of 3** \( \sqrt{3} \): ≈ 1.732050807...
- **Square root of 5** \( \sqrt{5} \): ≈ 2.236067977...
- **Golden ratio** \( \varphi \): ≈ 1.618033988... (\( (1 + \sqrt{5})/2 \))
- **Silver ratio** \( \sigma \): ≈ 2.414213562... (\( \sqrt{2} + 1 \))
- **Negative one**: −1
- **Cube root of 2** \( \sqrt[3]{2} \): ≈ 1.259921049...
- **Cube root of 3** \( \sqrt[3]{3} \): ≈ 1.442249570...
- **Twelfth root of 2** \( \sqrt[12]{2} \): ≈ 1.059463094...
- **Supergolden ratio** \( \psi \): ≈ 1.465571231...
- **Imaginary unit** \( i \): \( 0 + 1i \) (root of \( x^2 = -1 \))
- **Connective constant for hexagonal lattice** \( \mu \): ≈ 1.84... (and many more in continued fractions/sequences sections, plus specialized ones like Euler–Mascheroni \( \gamma \approx 0.57721 \), Apery's constant \( \zeta(3) \), Catalan's constant, Khinchin's constant, etc.)

Wikipedia's full mathematical constants page and related lists cover dozens more (e.g., in number theory, analysis). There is no fixed "total" as new ones are discovered.

### Notes on Other Sciences
- **Chemistry**: Overlaps heavily with physics (e.g., gas constant \( R \), Faraday constant). Compound-specific constants (e.g., acid dissociation \( K_a \) for thousands of acids) number in the thousands but are not "universal."
- **Biology/Earth Sciences/Astronomy**: Very few universal ones (e.g., Hubble parameter is time-dependent, not a true constant). Mostly empirical rates or derived from physics.
- **Total across all science**: Hundreds to low thousands if counting all named/used constants, but the core universal ones are the ~25–30 fundamental physics parameters + ~20–50 key mathematical ones.
