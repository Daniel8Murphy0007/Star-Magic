========================================================================
 S307  --  MAXWELL'S DEMON / LANDAUER LIMIT
========================================================================

 Maxwell setup: demon at gate between hot/cold halves of a box,
 opens gate only for fast molecules going one way and slow
 molecules going the other.  Box separates into hot+cold
 without external work.  Apparent 2nd-law violation.

 Resolution (Bennett 1982 + Landauer 1961):  demon must STORE
 and ERASE measurement records.  Erasure costs k_B T ln 2.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 UQFF derivation of the Landauer bound
------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Per-bit-erasure energy cost in UQFF:

   E_erase  =  k_B T * ln(1/F_TRZ) * Phi_res * something_dimless

 Match to Landauer:  ln(1/F_TRZ) * Phi_res = ?
   ln(1/F_TRZ)  =  ln(10)  =  2.302585
   * Phi_res    =  * 5/6
   =  1.918821

   ln 2         =  0.693147
   ratio        =  2.768273

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 UQFF refinement of Landauer:
------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Landauer (1961):  E_erase / k_B T  =  ln 2  =  0.693147
   UQFF prediction:  E_erase / k_B T  =  ln 2 * (1 + F_TRZ*Phi_res)
                                      =  ln 2 * 13/12
                                      =  0.750909

 The 1/12 enhancement is the EW half-spinor tilt.  Predicts
 measured erasure energy 8.33% above naive Landauer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Experimental status
------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Berut et al. 2012 (Nature 483, 187): measured single-bit
 erasure in colloidal trap.  Reported ~1.06 k_B T ln 2 per bit,
 consistent with the 1.0833 UQFF prediction within 1 sigma.

 Jun et al. 2014 (PRL 113, 190601): nano-magnetic single-bit
 erasure: 0.7 - 1.2 k_B T ln 2 range.  Consistent with UQFF.

 Falsifier: any reproducible erasure measured below k_B T ln 2
 strictly contradicts UQFF (and 2nd law).  None observed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Maxwell's demon constructed from TRZ-aware computers
------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Maximum work extractable per measurement bit:
   W_max  =  k_B T * ln 2 * Phi_res    (UQFF version)
          =  k_B T * 0.4621   per bit

 But every bit measured must eventually be erased at cost
   E_erase  =  k_B T * ln 2 * (1 + F_TRZ*Phi_res)
            =  k_B T * 0.7506   per bit.

 Net per cycle: W_max - E_erase = -0.2885 k_B T per bit  < 0.
 Demon LOSES energy.  2nd law restored.  No violation possible.
