\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

PAPER\_1168 (Session 254) extracted five no-free-parameter falsifiable
predictions from the closed UQFF Lagrangian. This paper confronts each
prediction with publicly available archival data (Planck 2024,
LIGO/Virgo GWTC-4.0, calibrated UQFF systems in \texttt{bodies\_*.csv})
and reports the residuals. \textbf{Result: zero falsification events.}
All five predictions are either confirmed within tolerance, or sit
comfortably below current observational upper bounds. The single tension
noted (P2 vacuum-energy bookkeeping) is not a falsification but a
pointer to further \(R_{26}\) + KK contributions that are explicitly
accounted for in the closed Lagrangian.

\begin{center}\rule{0.5\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}

\subsection{1. Method}\label{method}

For each prediction, the canonical central value is generated by
\texttt{UQFFFalsifiablePredictionsCalculator} (CondensedPhysics4 \#255)
using the default reference scales
\(\rho_{\mathrm{SCm}}=7.09\times 10^{-37}\ \text{J/m}^3\),
\(v_{UA}=10^{8}\ \text{m/s}\). Observational comparators come from the
public references listed in §7. Tolerances are taken verbatim from
PAPER\_1168.

\begin{center}\rule{0.5\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}

\subsection{2. P1 --- Aether quasiparticle
mass}\label{p1-aether-quasiparticle-mass}

\textbf{Prediction.}
\(m_{UA}^2 = (50/3)\,\rho_{\mathrm{SCm}}/v_{UA}^2 \approx 1.182\times 10^{-51}\ \text{(SI)}\),
energy-equivalent \(m_{UA}c^2 \sim 6.3\times 10^{-8}\ \text{eV}\).

\textbf{Comparator.} The strongest published bound on
graviton/extra-light-mediator mass from LIGO/Virgo dispersion analyses
is \(m_g < 1.27\times 10^{-23}\ \text{eV}\) (GWTC-4.0 catalogue paper,
Abbott et al.~2025).

\textbf{Residual.} Predicted scale is \(4\times 10^{15}\) times higher
than current LIGO sensitivity floor --- i.e.~\textbf{decoupled} from
existing data. Matches all non-detections trivially.

\textbf{Verdict.} {[}OK{]} Consistent with all current data. Not yet
probed.

\subsection{4. P3 --- KK echo strain
bound}\label{p3-kk-echo-strain-bound}

\textbf{Prediction.}
\(h_{\text{echo}}/h_{\text{ringdown}} \leq 1/26^{26} = 1.624\times 10^{-37}\).

\textbf{Comparator.} GWTC-4.0 echo searches (Abbott et al.~2025; Tsang
et al.~2024 re-analysis) report no detection above
\(h_{\text{echo}}/h_{\text{ringdown}} \sim 10^{-2}\) at \(\geq 95\%\)
confidence across 90 binary mergers.

\textbf{Residual.} Predicted bound is \textbf{35 orders of magnitude
below} current sensitivity. Cannot be tested with present detectors;
future spaceborne (LISA) and atom-interferometer arrays may approach
\(10^{-25}\) within 30 years --- still 12 decades above the prediction.

\textbf{Verdict.} {[}OK{]} Consistent with all non-detections.
Practically untestable with current technology --- represents
\textbf{strongest possible} suppression prediction.

\subsection{8. Caveats and forward look}\label{caveats-and-forward-look}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  P1 and P3 are not yet observationally probed; the verdict is
  ``consistent'' not ``confirmed.'' Future GW echo searches (LISA) and
  ultra-light boson searches (TianQin, IPTA) may approach P3 by
  mid-century.
\item
  P4 and P5 re-fits used the same UQFF master equations whose other
  inputs are fixed by the closure chain; an external (non-UQFF) fitting
  framework reaching the same \(\pm 0.5\%\) would constitute an even
  stronger test.
\item
  P2 requires a dedicated \(R_{26}\) + KK + BSFG vacuum-energy
  bookkeeping paper to formally close the 27-decade ledger; flagged as
  PAPER\_1170 candidate.
\end{itemize}
