\begin{abstract}
The UQFF framework encodes angular frequency inputs through $\omega_s$ (system) and $\omega_g$ (galactic). This paper provides the first direct observational calibration $\omega_{s,\rm galactic} = \sigma \times 10^3 / R_{\rm bulge}$ linking stellar velocity dispersion ($\sigma$) to the UQFF MUGE resonance channel, anchoring all galactic models to the M-$\sigma$ relation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Formula Derivation and Physical Basis}

\subsection{Dimensional Analysis}
The angular frequency $\omega$ has SI units of rad/s:
\[
[\omega] = \left[ \frac{v}{R} \right] = \frac{\rm m/s}{m} = {\rm s}^{-1}\ ({\rm rad/s}).
\]
Thus
\[
\omega_{s,\rm galactic} = \frac{\sigma}{R_{\rm bulge}}
\]
is dimensionally consistent when $\sigma$ is converted to m/s and $R_{\rm bulge}$ is in m.

\subsection{Master Calibration Formula}
\[
\omega_{s,\rm galactic} = \sigma \times 10^3 / R_{\rm bulge}
\]
where $\sigma$ is stellar velocity dispersion (km/s) and $R_{\rm bulge}$ is bulge radius (m).

\section{Conclusion}
This calibration directly bridges observable galactic kinematics (M-$\sigma$ relation) to the UQFF angular-frequency inputs used in all MUGE resonance and buoyancy calculations.
