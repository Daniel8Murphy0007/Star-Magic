# UQFF Reviewer Outreach List (Tier-4 N1)

**Purpose:** Identify 3-5 specific physicists to suggest as reviewers (in journal submission) AND to email directly for engagement.

**Strategy:** Cast a wide enough net that ONE responds positively. Cold-outreach response rate is typically 5-15%; aim for 5 emails to get 1-2 substantive responses.

## Cold outreach template (use BEFORE journal submission)

Send before the journal submission to potentially get a public endorsement or a private signal of interest. Subject: clear, technical, ~50 chars.

```

I am writing as the author of UQFF Star-Magic, a parameter-
economical physics framework that I have been developing
over the past two years. I am preparing a manuscript for
submission to Foundations of Physics and would value your
opinion before doing so.

The headline claim is a Bayesian Information Criterion
analysis showing Delta-BIC = 94.1 in favor of UQFF over
the Standard Model plus Lambda-CDM, purely on parameter-
economy grounds (9 free parameters vs 26). The framework
derives the cosmological constant Lambda at 0.003% match
to Planck, all 7 nuclear shell-model magic numbers exactly
from integer arithmetic, the Holmlid 630 eV LENR signature
exactly, and 200+ other named observables.

I am NOT asking you to endorse the framework or to
formally review it now. I am asking whether you would
be willing to:

1. Spend 30 minutes browsing https://github.com/Daniel8Murphy0007/Star-Magic
   and the CLOSURE_ATLAS.md, and tell me whether the
   framework warrants peer-review submission at all; or
2. Suggest a more appropriate venue if Foundations of
   Physics is not the right fit for this kind of work; or
3. Indicate that you would be willing to be cited as
   a suggested reviewer in the formal submission.

If you have any of those reactions in 30 minutes of looking,
that information would be valuable. If not, no reply needed
and I will not bother you again.

The package is `pip install uqff` and the 857-test fidelity
gate runs in under a minute via `uqff gate`. Cross-language
verification via C++ at 100% match.

Thank you for your time.

Respectfully,
Daniel T. Murphy
daniel.murphy00@enrgyone.com
```

## What to expect

- **Most will not reply.** That is normal; established physicists get 50+ cold emails per week.
- **Some will reply politely declining.** That is also normal.
- **One or two may reply with substantive critique.** That is gold; engage carefully, do NOT defensive, do iterate.
- **Zero are likely to immediately endorse.** That would be unusual; peer review takes time.

---

## Do NOT do

- Send to more than 5 people at once
- Send identical mass emails (each should reference something specific to that person's work)
- Follow up more than once after silence
- Promise things you cannot deliver
- Get into debates over email; redirect substantive discussions to GitHub issues

## Beyond the 5: secondary outreach

After Foundations of Physics submission, broader outreach options:

- **arXiv overlay:** post arXiv link to /r/Physics, /r/ParticlePhysics, Hacker News (carefully — these communities can be brutal but also surface engaged commenters)
- **Mastodon / Bluesky physics communities:** lower-stakes than reddit
- **Annual conferences:** APS April Meeting (high-energy, broad), GR-conferences (gravity), International Conference on Cold Fusion (LENR-focused, more receptive)
- **Specific working groups:** if you find a research group working on parameter-economy or alternative-cosmology, email the PI directly
- **University seminars:** offer to give a 30-min seminar at a physics department; some are open to alternative-physics talks
- **YouTube physics communicators:** Sabine Hossenfelder, Anton Petrov, PBS Spacetime — would not endorse but might mention the framework if approached respectfully

---

## Engagement boundaries

If reviewers / commenters become hostile or bad-faith:

- Do NOT engage past 2 substantive exchanges
- Do NOT escalate
- Do NOT publish their private email
- DO document the exchange privately for your records
- DO continue with formal peer review (the journal's editorial process exists to filter)

If a reviewer or commenter is **constructive**:

- Engage at length
- Cite their work in your manuscript
- Offer them co-authorship on follow-on papers if their contribution is substantial
- Build the relationship

---

## Realistic outcome

Of 5 cold emails sent:

- 0-1 substantive responses likely
- 1-2 polite acknowledgments
- 2-4 silent (default response)
- 0 immediate endorsements

Even 1 substantive response is a win. The point of outreach is to FIND the one person who's intellectually engaged, not to convince all 5.
